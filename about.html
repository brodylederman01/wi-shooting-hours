About WI Hunting Hours

WI Hunting Hours is an independent tool designed to help hunters find Wisconsin shooting hours by date and location.

How the tool works

Choose a hunting date and use your device location, search for a Wisconsin city or ZIP code, or select a point on the map. The tool uses local geographic data to identify the county and longitude zone, then applies the corresponding shooting-hour table.

City and ZIP results are approximate. Use your actual hunting coordinates when near a boundary. The site is not affiliated with the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources and is not a substitute for official regulations.

Official resources

Check the Wisconsin DNR hunting information for current regulations, seasons and official shooting-hour tables. Geographic data is based on Census TIGER/Line and GeoNames. Maps are provided through OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap data.
