How it works

Choose your hunting date, then use your device location, search for a Wisconsin city or ZIP, or select a point on the map. The tool determines your county, northern or southern area, and longitude zone.

Understanding Wisconsin hunting hours

Why do shooting hours vary by location?

Wisconsin publishes hunting-hour tables for northern and southern areas, with geographic time adjustments. This tool uses the selected county and longitude to choose the applicable table and zone. Legal shooting hours are not necessarily the same as sunrise and sunset.

Which location should I choose?

Use My Location at your hunting spot or select the exact spot on the map. City and ZIP search results represent approximate locations, which may fall on a different side of a county or zone boundary from your stand.

Can I check a future hunting date?

Choose the date first, then select your hunting location. Confirm that the date is covered by the current official tables and that the season, species and hunting method are permitted.

What if my location cannot be verified?

Try a nearby point within Wisconsin or search for a city or ZIP code. Do not rely on an unverified location for legal hunting times. Consult the Wisconsin DNR hunting regulations before hunting.

Always confirm regulations with the official Wisconsin DNR before hunting.
