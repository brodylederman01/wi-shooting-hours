Privacy Policy

Updated November 24, 2026.

This policy describes how WI Hunting Hours and the third-party services used on this website handle information.

Location

If you press Use My Location, your browser asks for permission to access your device location. The website uses coordinates in your browser to determine county, zone and shooting hours. You may deny or revoke permission through your browser settings. City and ZIP search uses a local dataset. Map requests to OpenFreeMap may reveal the approximate area displayed on your map and your IP address. The tool does not intentionally send precise GPS coordinates to Google Analytics or AdSense.

Analytics and advertising

We use Google Analytics to understand website usage and have added Google AdSense to support advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device identifiers and browsing information to measure usage, deliver ads and, where permitted, personalize advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on previous visits to this and other websites.

Learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can manage personalized ads through Google My Ad Center and manage cookies and location permissions through your browser. Where a privacy choices message is available, you may use it to manage your consent.

Hosting and maps

Hosting, map and library providers may process standard network information such as IP addresses, browser information and requested resources to deliver their services. See GitHub’s privacy statement and OpenFreeMap.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the website or its services change.
